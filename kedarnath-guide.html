🗓️ Recommended Tour Itinerary (3 Days / 2 Nights)

DAY 1

Rishikesh/Haridwar to Sonprayag (Drive: ~210 km)

Early morning Rishikesh/Haridwar se Sonprayag ke liye bus ya shared taxi lein. Raaste me Devprayag, Srinagar aur Rudraprayag ke scenic views milenge. Night stay Sonprayag ya Sitapur me karein.

DAY 2

Sonprayag → Gaurikund → Kedarnath Temple (Trek: 16 km)

Subah 5:00 AM Sonprayag se Gaurikund ke liye shared cab lein. Wahan se Kedarnath Dham ka trek start karein. Shaam tak Temple complex pahunch kar Evening Aarti attend karein. Night stay Kedarnath Mandir ke paas camps/dharamshala me karein.

DAY 3

Darshan & Trek Down to Sonprayag → Return Journey

Subah Morning Darshan aur Temple surroundings explore karne ke baad trek down Gaurikund ke liye start karein. Gaurikund se Sonprayag wapas aakar Rishikesh/Haridwar ke liye return transport lein.

Essential Yatra Tips

  • Char Dham Registration e-Pass zaroori hai (Uttarakhand Govt Portal se online karein).
  • Warm thermals, raincoat aur comfortable trekking shoes zaroor saath rakhein.
  • Network ke liye BSNL, Jio ya Airtel ka SIM card best connectivity deta hai.
