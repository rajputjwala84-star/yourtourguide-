Kedarnath Yatra Complete Guide: Kam Budget Me Safe Trip Kaise Karein

Updated 2026 5 min read

1. Kaise Pahuchein (How to Reach)?

Kedarnath jane ke liye sabse pehle aapko **Rishikesh** ya **Haridwar** pahunchna hota hai. Wahan se Sonprayag ke liye direct local buses ya shared taxis milti hain.

  • Nearest Railway Station: Rishikesh / Haridwar
  • Nearest Airport: Jolly Grant Airport, Dehradun
  • Base Camp: Sonprayag (Gaurikund tak shared cab milti hai)

2. Trek Route Details

Gaurikund se Kedarnath Temple tak ka trek lagbhag **16-18 km** ka hai. Raste me drinking water, tea stalls aur medical relief centers available rehte hain.

3. Estimated Budget (Per Person)

Haridwar to Sonprayag Bus: ₹400 - ₹600

Gaurikund Shared Cab: ₹50

Stay (Dharamshala/Camp): ₹500 - ₹1,200 per night

Food: ₹150 - ₹250 per meal

Total Estimated Budget: ₹3,500 - ₹5,000 (from Rishikesh)
