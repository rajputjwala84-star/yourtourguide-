About Meghalaya

Meghalaya ("Abode of Clouds") offers a unique experience. Walk on bio-engineered Living Root Bridges created over centuries by the Khasi tribe, swim in turquoise pools, and experience clouds entering your room in Cherrapunji.

4-Day Complete Itinerary

Day 1: Guwahati to Sohra Waterfalls

Drive via Umiam Lake, visit Nohkalikai & Seven Sisters Falls, stay in Sohra.

Day 2: Double Decker Root Bridge & Rainbow Falls

Trek down 3,000 steps to Nongriat, experience the iconic Double Decker Bridge, and visit Rainbow Falls.

Day 3: Dawki River & Mawlynnong Clean Village

Enjoy crystal-clear boating at Umngot River in Dawki, visit Asia's cleanest village, and head to Shillong.

Day 4: Mawsmai Cave & Return

Explore natural limestone caves in Mawsmai before departing back to Guwahati.
